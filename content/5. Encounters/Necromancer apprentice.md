##  Necromancer Apprentice — Spell Cards (Lvl 3 Caster)

---

####  **Necromancer Apprentice (CR 2)**

_Humanoid (Medium), Chaotic Neutral_

**HP:** 36  
**AC:** 13 (Mage Armor active)  
**Speed:** 30 ft

**Spells:**

- _Cantrips:_ Chill Touch, Toll the Dead, Mage Hand
    
- _1st level (4 slots):_ Shield, Mage Armor, Magic Missile
    
- _2nd level (2 slots):_ Ray of Enfeeblement, Mirror Image
    

**Special:**

- **Rooted Channel (Recharge 5–6):** Calls on the corruption.
    
    - Necrotic vines erupt in 15 ft radius (DC 13 DEX save, 2d8 necrotic, half on success).
        
    - Area becomes _difficult terrain_ until the end of next round.

###  **Cantrip: Chill Touch**

**Range:** 120 ft  
**To Hit:** +5  
**Hit:** 2d8 necrotic  
**Effect:** Target can’t regain HP until start of necromancer’s next turn.  
**Flavor:** Skeletal hand bursts from the ground, clutching the victim.

---

###  **Cantrip: Toll the Dead**

**Range:** 60 ft  
**Save:** WIS 13  
**Fail:** 1d8 necrotic (1d12 if target is missing HP)  
**Flavor:** Eerie bell echoes from underground roots.

---

###  **Cantrip: Mage Hand**

**Range:** 30 ft  
**Effect:** Spectral hand can interact with small objects or trigger traps.  
**Flavor:** Blackened ghostly hand forms from the necromancer’s shadow.

---

###  **1st Level: Shield**

**Casting Time:** Reaction (when hit by attack or targeted by Magic Missile)  
**Effect:** +5 AC until start of next turn.  
**Auto-negates Magic Missile.**  
**Flavor:** Translucent necrotic barrier flares up from his amulet.

---

###  **1st Level: Mage Armor**

**Duration:** 8 hours  
**Target:** Self  
**Effect:** AC = 13 + DEX mod (already active).  
**Flavor:** Faint green shimmer around his robe.

---

###  **1st Level: Magic Missile**

**Range:** 120 ft  
**Effect:** 3 darts (1d4 + 1 force each), auto-hit.  
**Targets:** One or several.  
**Flavor:** Cracks of green necrotic light shoot from his fingertips.

---

###  **2nd Level: Ray of Enfeeblement**

**Range:** 60 ft  
**To Hit:** +5  
**Hit:** Target deals half damage with STR-based weapon attacks (Con save each turn ends effect).  
**Flavor:** Sickly black energy drains strength from the victim’s limbs.

---

###  **2nd Level: Mirror Image**

**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Duration:** 1 minute  
**Effect:** Creates 3 illusory duplicates.  
Each time he’s targeted by an attack, roll to see if it hits an illusion.  
**Flavor:** Three corrupted versions of him flicker and blend into the roots.

---

###  **Special Ability: Rooted Channel (Recharge 5–6)**

**Effect:** 15 ft radius necrotic burst; DC 13 DEX save, 2d8 necrotic (half on success).  
Area becomes difficult terrain (writhing roots).  
**Flavor:** The corruption beneath the ground lashes upward, hungry for life.
