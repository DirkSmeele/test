# 🐺 Verdant Wolf (CR 1/2)
_Medium beast (corrupted), unaligned_
**AC** 13  
**HP** 18 (4d8)  
**Speed** 40 ft

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|3 (-4)|12 (+1)|6 (-2)|

**Saving Throws** Dex +4  
**Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +4  
**Senses** passive Perception 13  
### Traits
**Pack Tactics**  
The wolf has advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the wolf’s allies is within 5 ft of the creature.
**Verdant Resilience**  
The wolf has advantage on saving throws against being **frightened** or **charmed**.

---

### Actions

**Bite.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft  
Hit: **7 (2d4 + 2) piercing damage**  
➕ Target must succeed on a **DC 11 Con save** or take **2 necrotic damage** at the start of its next turn.

## 🐺 Verdant Alpha Wolf (CR ~2)

_Medium beast (corrupted), unaligned_
**AC** 14  
**HP** 55 (7d8 + 21)  
**Speed** 50 ft

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|5 (-3)|14 (+2)|8 (-1)|

**Saving Throws** Con +5, Wis +4  
**Skills** Perception +4, Intimidation +3  
**Senses** passive Perception 14

---

### Traits

**Pack Commander (Aura)**  
All allied wolves within 20 ft gain:

- +1 to attack rolls
- Advantage on first attack each round

**Corrupt Howl (Recharge 5–6)**  
As a bonus action, the Alpha howls.  
All enemies within 15 ft must make a **DC 13 Wis save** or be **frightened** of the Alpha until the end of their next turn.

---

### Actions

**Multiattack.**  
The Alpha makes **two Bite attacks**.

**Bite.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit  
Hit: **9 (2d6 + 3) piercing damage**  
➕ DC 13 Con save or take **3 necrotic damage** and have **disadvantage on their next Con save**.

---
### Reaction
**Punishing Snap (1/round)**  
When a creature within 5 ft hits the Alpha, it makes a Bite attack against that creature.
