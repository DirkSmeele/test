## 💀 2️⃣ Elite skeletons (CR 1)

**Gebruik 1–2 als bodyguards of mini-luitenants**

### **Bone Knight**

- **CR:** 1
    
- **HP:** 45 (6d8+18)
    
- **AC:** 16 (rusty plate)
    
- **Attack:** Longsword +4 to hit, 1d8+2 slashing
    
- **Special:**
    
    - _Undying Loyalty:_ When the necromancer takes damage, can use reaction to interpose itself (damage transfer).
        
    - _Aura of Dread (1/rest):_ 10 ft radius, DC 13 WIS save or frightened until end of next turn.
        
- **Flavor:** Once a loyal guard, now bound in unholy chains, eyes glowing faint green.

### **Skeleton Archer**

- **CR:** 1/2
    
- **HP:** 22
    
- **AC:** 13
    
- **Attack:** Shortbow +4 to hit, 1d6+2 piercing
    
- **Tactics:** Keeps distance, focuses on casters.
    
- **Flavor:** Rotted quiver, arrows made from blackened vines

### **Boneblade Skeleton**

- **CR:** 1/2
    
- **HP:** 27 (6d8)
    
- **AC:** 14 (bone armor)
    
- **Speed:** 30 ft
    
- **Attack:** Multiattack (2 × Shortsword +4 to hit, 1d6+2 piercing)
    
- **Special:**
    
    - _Bone Shards (Reaction):_ When reduced to 0 HP, explodes in shards; DC 12 DEX save or take 1d6 slashing.
        
- **Flavor:** A skeleton whose bones are sharpened and fused with obsidian-like corruption.
