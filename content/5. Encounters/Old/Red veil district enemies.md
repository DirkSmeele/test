**Abilities / Traits**

- **Blood Rage:** Gains +2 melee damage for 1 minute after taking damage ≥ 15 HP in one turn.
- **Escapes Easily:** Can Disengage as a bonus action and climb vertical surfaces with Athletics check DC 14.
- **Blood Veil (Recharge 5–6):** Creates 15 ft radius zone; creatures in zone take 1d6 necrotic damage per turn; visibility halved.

**Actions**

- **Greataxe Attack:** +8 to hit, 2d12+5 slashing
- **Multiattack:** Two greataxe swings
- **Throw Vial:** Blood Ore explosive, 15 ft range, Dex DC13 save, 2d8 fire/necrotic

**Tactics / Role in Fight:**
- Avoids direct death until final chase.
- Uses Blood Veil to slow party, throws vials to damage/block paths.
