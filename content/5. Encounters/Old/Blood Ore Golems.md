# 🗿 BLOODBRUTE GOLEM
_Large construct, unaligned_
**Armor Class** 16 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 130 (15d10 + 45)  
**Speed** 20 ft
### **Abilities**

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|18 (+4)|10 (+0)|17 (+3)|3 (−4)|10 (+0)|5 (−3)|

---

### **Saving Throws**

- **STR +6**
- **CON +5**
### **Damage Resistances**
Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks
### **Damage Vulnerabilities**
Thunder
### **Damage Immunities**
Poison
### **Condition Immunities**
Charmed, Exhaustion, Poisoned
### **Senses**
Darkvision 60 ft, passive Perception 10
### **Challenge**
CR 4 (1,100 XP)

---
## **Actions**

### **Multiattack** 2x
### **Crushing Slam**
_Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 10 ft, one target  
_Hit:_ 11 (1d12 + 5) bludgeoning damage.  
Target must succeed on a **DC 14 Strength saving throw** or be **knocked prone**.

---

## **Reactions**

### **Blood Pulse (Recharge 5–6)**

When the golem has 3 **Blood Resonance** charges, it releases a pulse of corrupted energy.  
Creatures within 10 ft must make a **DC 14 Constitution saving throw**:
- Fail: 10 (2d6) necrotic damage and speed becomes 0 until the end of their next turn
- Success: Half damage, no speed reduction
---
## **Traits**
### **Blood Resonance**
Whenever a creature within 10 ft of the golem takes damage, the golem gains 1 charge (max 3).

# 🗿 BLOODSPIKE GOLEM
_Large construct, unaligned_
**Armor Class** 16 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points** 100 (13d10 + 39)  
**Speed** 20 ft
### **Abilities**

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|3 (−4)|11 (+0)|5 (−3)|

---
### **Saving Throws**
- **DEX +4**
- **CON +5**
CR 4 (1,100 XP)
## **Actions**

### **Multiattack** 2x

### **Bloodspike Volley**
_Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +6 to hit, range 60 ft, one target  
_Hit:_ 11 (1d10 + 3 piercing + 1d6 necrotic damage).
Target must succeed on a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw** or be **Impaled**.

---
## **Impaled (Condition)**

- Speed becomes 0
- Takes 2 (1d4) necrotic damage at the start of its turn
- Ends if the creature uses an action to succeed on a **DC 12 Strength check**, or receives magical healing
---
## **Actions (Recharge)**

### **Spine Burst (Recharge 5–6)**

The golem releases a spray of spikes in a 15-foot cone.  
Creatures in the area must make a **DC 14 Dexterity saving throw**:
- Fail: 10 (3d6) piercing damage and pushed 10 ft
- Success: Half damage, no push

# Rust Monster

Medium monstrosity, Unaligned

- Armor Class 14 (Natural Armor)
- Hit Points 27 (5d8+5)
- Speed 40 ft.

STR  13 (+1) | DEX 12 (+1) | CON 13 (+1) | INT  2 (-4) | WIS  3 (+1) | CHA  6 (-2)| 

Melee 
+3 / d8+1 piercing

- Senses Darkvision 60 Ft., passive Perception 11
- Challenge 1/2 (100 XP)
- Iron Scent. The rust monster can pinpoint, by scent, the location of ferrous metal within 30 feet of it.
- Rust Metal. Any nonmagical weapon made of metal that hits the rust monster corrodes. After dealing damage, the weapon takes a permanent and cumulative -1 penalty to damage rolls. If its penalty drops to -5, the weapon is destroyed. Non magical ammunition made of metal that hits the rust monster is destroyed after dealing damage.
