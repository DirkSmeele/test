### 🌹 **Chromatic Rose of Sylvarra**

_Wondrous Item, rare (requires attunement)_

**Description:**  
A crystal flower with shifting hues of violet, green, and pale silver. The petals pulse faintly, as though breathing. The rose hums softly near sources of necrotic energy — both drawn to it and repelled by it.

---

#### **While attuned:**

- You have **resistance to necrotic damage.**
    
- You have **advantage on Constitution saving throws** against necrotic effects or sources of corruption (disease, rot, or exhaustion).
    
- Once per **long rest**, when you or a creature within 10 feet would take necrotic damage, you can use your **reaction** to absorb part of that energy.
    
    - Reduce the damage by **1d8**, and the chosen creature regains that many hit points instead.
        
    - The rose’s petals blacken for a moment before regaining their color.
        

---

#### 🌑 **Corruption’s Fragility**

Whenever you take **necrotic damage** while attuned to the rose, roll a **d6**.

- On a **1**, the rose **wilts** for the rest of the day.
    
- While wilted, the rose’s colors fade, and **its magic becomes dormant** until you finish a **long rest**.
